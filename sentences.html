Rihla Sentence Quest

Six familiar topics, from words to full sentences. Learn each topic in the practice room first, pass its check, then defeat the Scramblers to open the gate.

The journey · practise, then play
How it works
1. LearnIn the practice room, tap any word or sentence to hear it. Read the questions and answers out loud.
2. Pass the checkAnswer 10 questions about the topic. Get 8 right to unlock that level.
3. PlayArrow keys or A D to move, Space to jump. Touch a Scrambler to get a question. Press 1–4 to answer.
4. Build sentencesIn the harder levels, tap the words in the right order to build the sentence. Arabic reads right to left.

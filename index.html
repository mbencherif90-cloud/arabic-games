Rihla Letter Quest

Scramblers have mixed up the sounds of the desert. Run, jump and read Arabic sounds to defeat them and open each oasis gate.

The journey · 5 levels, getting harder
How to play
Move and jumpArrow keys or A D to move, Space or ↑ to jump. On a tablet, use the buttons under the game.
Touch a ScramblerYou get an Arabic phonics question. Answer correctly to defeat it. A wrong answer costs a heart. Press 1–4 to answer.
Open the gateDefeat every Scrambler, then reach the gate. Lose no hearts for 3 stars.
Stuck?The practice room has the same questions with no enemies, no timer and no hearts to lose.
